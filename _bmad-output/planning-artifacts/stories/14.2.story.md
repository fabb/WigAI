# Story 14.2: Groove, Transport Options and Cue Mix

**Epic:** [Epic 14: Project-wide Control](../prd/epic-14.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to set groove and transport options.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   `set_groove` sets groove on/off, shuffle and accent amount, rate and phase.
*   `set_transport_options` sets punch in/out, metronome volume, pre-roll, automation write mode, launcher overdub, default launch quantization and post-recording action, and runs rewind, fast forward, tap tempo, continue or record.
*   `set_cue` sets cue volume and cue mix.
*   `set_track` gains `group_expanded` and `crossfade_mode`.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `set_groove`, `set_transport_options`, `set_cue`.
