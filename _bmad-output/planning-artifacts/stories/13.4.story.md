# Story 13.4: Reading and Editing Notes

**Epic:** [Epic 13: Arrangement, Scenes, Clips and Notes](../prd/epic-13.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to read the notes of a clip and change them in place.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   `read_notes` returns every note (start and duration in beats, pitch, velocity, channel) and every expression or condition that is not at its default.
*   `edit_notes` changes all notes or those matching a pitch and/or start range: velocity, release velocity, velocity spread, duration, gain, pan, timbre, pressure, micro-pitch, chance, occurrence, recurrence (length and mask), mute, repeats with curve and velocity shape.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `read_notes`, `edit_notes`.

### Implementation Notes

*   Notes are read through `addNoteStepObserver` on a launcher cursor clip (16384 x 128 steps of 1/16 beat). The note map must not be cleared after `selectChannel`, or notes delivered by it are lost.
