# Story 11.2: Describe a Device, Scanning on Demand

**Epic:** [Epic 11: Device Knowledge](../prd/epic-11.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to know what each control of a device does, measured rather than guessed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   `describe_device` returns the stored facts; without a stored scan (or with `rescan`) it measures the controls now, on a given instance (values restored afterwards) or on a temporary track that is deleted.
*   A scan sweeps each control and records its range, unit and options; every scan is kept in a history with what changed since the previous one.
*   Scans are refused while the transport plays.
*   `catalog_status` reports counts, recent scans, stale scans (older Bitwig version) and failures.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `describe_device`, `catalog_status`.

### Implementation Notes

*   The catalog is `~/.local/share/wigai/device-catalog.json` (or `$WIGAI_CATALOG_DIR`).
