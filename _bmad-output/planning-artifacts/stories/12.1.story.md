# Story 12.1: Engine, Meters and Playing Notes

**Epic:** [Epic 12: Listening and Auditioning](../prd/epic-12.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to know whether and what a track is playing.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   `set_engine_active` activates the audio engine of the current project (only one open project has it).
*   `get_levels` returns peak and RMS for every track and the master.
*   `get_playing_notes` returns the notes sounding on each track.
*   `get_device_parameters` adds `modulated_value`/`modulated_display` for controls a modulator is moving.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `set_engine_active`, `get_levels`, `get_playing_notes`.

### Implementation Notes

*   Meters use `addVuMeterObserver` per track; playing notes use `Channel.playingNotes()`.
