# Story 12.2: Audition and MIDI

**Epic:** [Epic 12: Listening and Auditioning](../prd/epic-12.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to play notes and send MIDI to an instrument to try a sound.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   `play_notes` plays timed notes into a track's instrument without a clip.
*   `send_midi` sends CC, pitch bend, program change, aftertouch or a raw channel message; values are range-checked.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `play_notes`, `send_midi`.

### Implementation Notes

*   Notes are scheduled with `host.scheduleTask` and `Track.startNote`/`stopNote`; MIDI goes through `Track.sendMidi`.
