# Story 13.3: Clip Management

**Epic:** [Epic 13: Arrangement, Scenes, Clips and Notes](../prd/epic-13.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to copy, move, duplicate and record clips.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   Clips can be copied or moved to an empty slot on any track and duplicated into the next free slot.
*   `record_into_slot` starts recording into an armed track's slot.
*   `set_clip` sets name, loop on/off, loop start and length, play start and stop, shuffle, accent (0.5 = none) and color.
*   `transform_clip` transposes, quantizes, duplicates content and sets launch quantization and mode.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `copy_clip`, `move_clip`, `duplicate_clip`, `record_into_slot`, `set_clip`, `transform_clip`.
