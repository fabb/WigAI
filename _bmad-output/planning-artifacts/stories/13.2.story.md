# Story 13.2: Scenes

**Epic:** [Epic 13: Arrangement, Scenes, Clips and Notes](../prd/epic-13.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to manage scenes.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   Scenes can be created, renamed, colored, duplicated, deleted, and have all their clips copied or moved onto another scene's row; results list every scene with its clip count.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `create_scene`, `rename_scene`, `set_scene`, `duplicate_scene`, `copy_scene`, `move_scene`, `delete_scene`.

### Implementation Notes

*   Copy and move use the target scene's `replaceInsertionPoint()`; duplicate uses `duplicateObject()`.
