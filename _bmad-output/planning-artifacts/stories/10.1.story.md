# Story 10.1: Chains Inside Devices

**Epic:** [Epic 10: Device Chains, Presets and Containers](../prd/epic-10.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to read and edit the chains inside a device (slots and layers), so I can build a vocoder or a layered sound.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   `get_device_chains` lists every slot (`Modulator`, `Carrier`, `FX`, ...) and layer (`layer:N`) of a device with the devices in each.
*   `insert_device`, `copy_device`/`move_device`, `delete_device`, `set_device_enabled`, `get_device_parameters` and `set_device_parameters` accept `chain` (+ `chain_device_index`) to work on a device inside a chain; copy/move take `from_chain`/`to_chain`.
*   Moving a device between two chains of the same container works.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `get_device_chains`.

### Implementation Notes

*   Slots are browsed one at a time with `CursorDeviceSlot.selectSlot(name)`; the selected slot belongs to the device, not the cursor, so moving between two slots of the same device uses the cursor itself as the source.
*   Layers each get a `DeviceBank` created at init.
