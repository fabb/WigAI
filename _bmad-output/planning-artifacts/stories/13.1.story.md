# Story 13.1: Arrangement and Markers

**Epic:** [Epic 13: Arrangement, Scenes, Clips and Notes](../prd/epic-13.md)

**User Story:**

*   As an AI agent, I want to navigate and loop the arrangement.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

*   Cue markers are listed with positions and can be jumped to by index or name.
*   `set_arrangement` sets the loop range in beats and the time signature.
*   `stop_track` stops a track's launcher clip or returns it to the arrangement.
*   `api-reference.md` documents `list_cue_markers`, `go_to_cue_marker`, `set_arrangement`, `stop_track`.
